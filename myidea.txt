a dream

the islands represent your mistakes as a person

you find each island one by one, in order

each island reveals your mistakes, each one gets worse and the atmosphere and ambient around you gets more darker, as if someone's watching you and you struggle more to sail your boat.

island 2: Did you deserve it?

island 3: You were just lucky.

island 4: Why are you crying?

island 5: You caused all of this.

island 6: You never tried.

island 7: It's all your fault.
